6. Motion Design Presets

Eight deterministic animation algorithms that map 1:1 between canvas preview and ASS/FFmpeg render output.

Fade through darkness ASS: \fad(400,300)

Smooth cinematic opacity interpolation with zero harsh cuts.

Blur into focus ASS: \blur(8-0)

Optical lens pull from heavy gaussian diffusion into crisp legibility.

Slow cinematic rise ASS: \pos(Y+20->Y)

Subtle upward gravitational drift with cubic ease-out.

Tracking reveal ASS: \fsp(16->4)

Kerning expansion that settles into tight editorial rhythm.

Type-on ASS: Character reveal

Rhythmic typewriter character apparition synchronized to syllables.

Word-by-word highlight ASS: \k syllable tags

Progressive vocal wipe highlighting words as they are vocalized.

Subtle scale and drift ASS: \fscx / \fscy(96->100)

Gentle 4% scale breathing that keeps static shots alive.

Beat-reactive pulse ASS: Transient scale burst

Rhythmic scaling burst coupled to detected low-frequency audio transients.
