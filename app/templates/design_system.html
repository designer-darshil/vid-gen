LyricStudio Design System

Vibe-Coded Creative Workspace v3.0

An immersive, aesthetic dark design system for motion design, music visualization, and animated lyrics video authoring. Features near-black charcoal surfaces, electric-lavender accents, and a strict 16px minimum font size across all interfaces.

5. Animated Lyric Templates (7 Signature Styles)

Each template defines a unique visual motion treatment, animated typography, and ASS/FFmpeg render pipeline integration.

MEMORIES DRIFT
1. Cinematic Letterbox
Font: Cinzel • Animation: Fade-Slide + Drift

Filmic 2.39:1 widescreen letterbox bars, elegant serif tracking, deep drop shadow, and slow drift scale.

ELECTRIC NIGHTS
2. Neon Cyber Bloom
Font: Outfit • Animation: Pulse Glow Bloom

Multi-stage neon gas tube glow with chromatic outer bloom, pulse frequency, and energetic neon core.

SING ALONG NOW
3. Karaoke Word Highlight
Font: Outfit • Animation: Syllable Wipe (\k tags)

Real-time word-by-word progressive wipe highlighting synchronized directly with the singer's vocal delivery.

PURITY & FORM
4. Minimal Swiss Sans
Font: Inter / Geist • Animation: Smooth Vertical Slide

Geometric sans-serif typography with clean tracking, subtle upward slide, and zero visual clutter.

ETHEREAL SKY
5. Dreamy Gentle Wave
Font: Playfair Display • Animation: Floating Wave Drift

Soft lavender-violet halo, undulating gentle bob motion, and luminous breathing ambient drift.

DYNAMIC BEAT
6. Kinetic Expressive Pop
Font: Space Grotesk / Syne • Animation: Overshoot Pop Bounce

High-energy expressive typography with punchy scale bounce and dynamic entrance movement on phrase changes.

SOUND WAVE
7. Music Visualizer Audio Reactive
Font: Outfit • Animation: Audio Spectrum Waves

Dynamic reactive frequency equalizer bars pulsing rhythmically below synchronized typography.

WCAG AA compliance guidelines and strict design system invariants.
